# Product requirements — initial scope

## Customer
- Register, sign in and manage basic profile/address
- Browse categories and products
- View product details and availability
- Add/update/remove cart items
- Submit a cash-on-delivery order
- View order history and current status

## Staff/admin
- Sign in with staff permissions
- Create/update/archive products and categories
- Update stock
- View and filter orders
- Update order status and assign delivery details manually

## Explicitly deferred
- Multi-vendor marketplace
- Dedicated rider application
- Live GPS tracking
- Online payment gateway
- Automated dispatch/route optimization
- Complex promotions/loyalty
